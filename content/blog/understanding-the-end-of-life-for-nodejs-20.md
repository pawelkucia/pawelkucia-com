## What Does Node.js 20 EOL Mean?
Node.js 20 has officially reached end-of-life status, which means it no longer receives updates or security patches. As a result, major platforms like GitHub Actions have removed Node.js 20 from their supported environments, and Vercel is ending its support as well.

## Impact on Developers and Projects
This change affects continuous integration workflows and deployment processes that rely on Node.js 20. Using an unsupported version can lead to security vulnerabilities and compatibility issues with newer dependencies or frameworks.

## Preparing Your Projects for Change
To maintain a stable and secure development environment, it's essential to upgrade your projects to a maintained Node.js version. Review your CI/CD configurations on GitHub Actions, update your Node.js runtime in your projects, and test thoroughly before deployment.

## Conclusion
Staying current with supported Node.js versions ensures your projects benefit from the latest performance improvements and security fixes. Proactively managing these transitions is key to maintaining reliable development and deployment pipelines.
