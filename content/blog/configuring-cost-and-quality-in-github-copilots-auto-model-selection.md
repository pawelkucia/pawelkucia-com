## Balancing Cost, Quality, and Response Time in Copilot

GitHub Copilot now introduces an enhanced auto model selection mechanism, offering three distinct tiers: **efficiency**, **balance**, and **intelligence**. This update gives developers granular control over how the AI assistant prioritizes between cost management, output quality, and speed.

## What the Tiers Mean

- **Efficiency**: Optimizes for lower cost and faster responses, ideal for tasks where quick feedback is crucial but ultra-high precision is less important.
- **Balance**: A middle ground that weighs cost and quality equally, suitable for general coding tasks where moderate speed and accuracy are desirable.
- **Intelligence**: Maximizes output quality, perfect for complex scenarios requiring more detailed and accurate suggestions, accepting higher computational cost.

## Why This Matters

This tiered approach reflects the growing need for customizable AI tools that adapt to diverse development workflows and budgets. By allowing developers to select the model tier that aligns with their project priorities, Copilot enhances productivity and efficiency.

As AI continues to integrate deeper into software engineering, such features pave the way for smarter, more economical coding assistance.
