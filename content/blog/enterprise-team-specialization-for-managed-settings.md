## Tackling Governance at Scale

Managing IT policies across large enterprises presents challenges, particularly when every configuration change depends on central administrators. This often results in bottlenecks and one-size-fits-all settings that don’t meet diverse team needs.

## Introducing Team-Specific Managed Settings

Enterprise administrators now have the ability to customize managed settings by targeting specific enterprise teams. This is achieved through itemized configuration files tailored for each team, enabling granular control over governance policies.

## Benefits of Specialization

- **Scalability:** Teams receive appropriate settings without waiting for central approval delays.
- **Flexibility:** Different teams can have configurations designed specifically for their workflows.
- **Efficiency:** Reduces administrative overhead and streamlines governance across complex organizations.

This advancement supports enterprise agility by aligning governance with the natural structure of organizations rather than forcing uniform policies.
