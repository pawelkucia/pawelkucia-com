## What is Agentic Autofix?
Agentic autofix leverages an agent-based approach to remediate code scanning alerts automatically. Instead of simple one-to-one fixes, it navigates your codebase similarly to how a developer would—reviewing relevant files and contexts before suggesting a fix.

## Why It Matters
Traditional autofixes are often limited in scope or risk introducing errors. Agentic autofix improves accuracy and applicability by understanding the broader code context. This results in safer and more effective remediation of security or quality issues.

## Benefits for Development Teams
- Reduced manual effort in fixing alerts
- Faster turnaround on vulnerability fixes
- Increased confidence in applied changes

## Conclusion
The general availability of agentic autofix marks a significant step toward smarter, context-aware automation in code security and quality workflows. It empowers teams to maintain healthier codebases with less friction.
