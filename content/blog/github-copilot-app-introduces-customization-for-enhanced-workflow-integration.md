## Customize Copilot to Fit Your Team’s Workflow

GitHub Copilot, the AI pair programmer, just became more adaptable to your team’s unique setup. With the introduction of the Customize tab, developers can now fine-tune Copilot’s behavior to better align with the tools, knowledge, and workflows their team relies on every day.

## Why Customization Matters

Most development teams have established ways of working, preferred coding styles, and specialized tools. AI assistance that adapts to these specifics can be more effective, reduce friction, and improve code quality, helping teams deliver faster while maintaining control.

## What This Means for Developers

The Customize tab allows you to configure settings that influence how Copilot generates suggestions, helping it understand your team’s coding preferences and project context better. This integration ensures that AI support feels less intrusive and more like a natural extension of your workflow.

By empowering teams to personalize their AI tools, GitHub Copilot moves one step closer to becoming an effective partner in complex software development environments.
