## Seamless Multi-Repository Collaboration

GitHub Copilot’s integration with Slack and Microsoft Teams has been enhanced to support working across multiple repositories in a single conversation. This means developers can now track pull requests and investigate issues spanning various projects without switching channels or contexts.

## Why Multi-Repo Support Matters

Managing multiple repositories often involves jumping between different discussion threads and tools, which can disrupt focus and slow down collaboration. By consolidating this information in one place, teams can streamline workflows and improve communication efficiency.

## Improving Developer Experience

This update reflects an ongoing effort to integrate development workflows directly into platforms where teams already communicate. It reduces friction and enables faster responses to code changes and issues.

As modern development increasingly relies on diverse repositories and distributed teams, such improvements can significantly impact productivity and coordination.
