## Simplifying Copilot Model Enablement

GitHub has announced a new global default enablement policy for Copilot models applicable to Business and Enterprise plans. This significant update aims to remove the need for admins to manually enable each new Copilot model.

## What This Means for Organizations

With the default enablement policy, teams can leverage new Copilot AI models immediately upon release without additional configuration. This streamlines AI adoption, reducing administrative overhead and accelerating developer productivity.

## Enhancing AI Integration in Development

By making Copilot models generally available by default, GitHub supports seamless integration of AI assistance into everyday workflows. Organizations benefit from faster access to advanced tools while maintaining scalable management.

This enhancement reflects GitHub's commitment to evolving developer experiences through intelligent automation and enterprise-friendly solutions.
